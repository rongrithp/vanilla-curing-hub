## Hardware & Optical Environment - Color Calibration Box
* **Dimensions & Enclosure:** Standardized sealed light box to isolate external ambient lux variations.
* **Illumination Specification:** Controlled CRI ≥ 95 LED arrays, uniform diffuse lighting, calibrated color temperature (5000K / D50 equivalent).
* **Reference Target:** Standard 24-patch ColorChecker chart placed at a fixed focal plane relative to the camera lens.
* **Optical Constraints:** Fixed focal length, fixed working distance, locked exposure, fixed ISO, and manual white balance lock to prevent dynamic gain drift.
